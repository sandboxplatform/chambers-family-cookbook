# Appetizers
@ Almond Cheddar Appetizers
> These warm, cheesy bites are perfect for parties or gatherings. The combination of crispy almonds, bacon, and cheddar on toasted baguette slices makes for a delightful appetizer that's both savory and satisfying.
- 1 cup mayonnaise (not Miracle Whip)
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- ¾ cup slivered almonds
- 6 strips bacon, crumbled
- 1 baguette
= Preheat the Oven
* Set the oven to 400°F.
= Prepare the Mixture
* In a mixing bowl, combine the mayonnaise, cheddar cheese, chopped onion, slivered almonds, and crumbled bacon. Mix well.
= Assemble the Appetizers
* Slice the baguette into ½-inch thick rounds.
* Spread a generous amount of the mixture onto each slice.
= Bake
* Place the topped baguette slices on a baking sheet.
* Bake at 400°F for 8 minutes, or until the cheese is bubbly and slightly golden.
= Serve
* Let cool slightly before serving. Enjoy these crispy, cheesy bites warm!
. Perfect for entertaining, these appetizers will disappear fast!

@ Guacamole
> This fresh and flavorful guacamole is perfect for dipping, spreading on toast, or serving as a topping for tacos and burritos. Made with ripe avocados, juicy tomatoes, and a hint of spice, it's a simple yet delicious dish that can be customized to taste.
- 4 ripe avocados
- 1 small amount of jalapeño (about ¼ diced)
- ½ medium onion, chopped
- 2 fresh tomatoes, chopped
- Salt to taste
- (Optional) 2 tablespoons of oil
= Prepare the Ingredients
* Cut the avocados in half, remove the pits, and scoop the flesh into a bowl.
* Dice the jalapeño finely (use more or less depending on spice preference).
* Chop the onion and tomatoes into small chunks.
= Mix Everything Together
* Lightly mash the avocados with a fork, keeping some chunks for texture.
* Add the jalapeño, onion, and tomatoes.
* Sprinkle with salt to taste and mix well.
= Final Touches
* If desired, add 2 tablespoons of oil for a smoother texture.
* Serve immediately with tortilla chips, tacos, or as a topping for your favorite dishes.
. Enjoy your homemade guacamole!

@ Crabmeat Dip
> This creamy and flavorful crabmeat dip is perfect for parties, gatherings, or as a delicious appetizer. The combination of cream cheese, crab, and a touch of sherry creates a rich and satisfying dip.
- 8 oz cream cheese
- 1 teaspoon sherry
- 6 ½ oz crabmeat
- 2 tablespoons chopped onion
- ½ teaspoon horseradish
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- Pepper (to taste)
- Slivered almonds (for topping)
= Prepare the Mixture
* In a saucepan over low heat, blend the cream cheese, sherry, crabmeat, onion, horseradish, salt, and pepper.
* Stir continuously until smooth and well combined.
= Bake the Dip
* Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
* Transfer the mixture to a casserole dish.
* Sprinkle slivered almonds on top.
= Final Baking
* Bake for 15 minutes until warm and bubbly.
= Serve & Enjoy
* Serve hot with crackers, toasted bread, or fresh vegetables for dipping.
. This delicious crabmeat dip is a perfect addition to any appetizer spread!

@ Shrimp Tarragon
> This elegant shrimp dish features a rich, creamy tarragon sauce with a touch of cognac for a bold and flavorful finish. Perfect for a special dinner, it pairs beautifully with rice or crusty bread.
- 5 tablespoons butter
- 36 medium-sized shrimp, peeled and deveined
- Salt (to taste)
- 2 tablespoons finely minced shallots
- ½ teaspoon tarragon
- Black pepper (to taste)
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- 3 tablespoons warm cognac
- ¾ cup heavy cream, heated
- 1 tablespoon flour
= Sauté the Shrimp
* Melt 4 tablespoons of butter in a pan over medium heat.
* Add the shrimp in a single layer and sprinkle with salt.
* Cook for about 4 minutes, stirring occasionally, until shrimp are pink and opaque.
= Add Flavor
* Sprinkle the shrimp with lemon juice, shallots, tarragon, and black pepper.
* Stir to combine.
= Flambé with Cognac
* Sprinkle the warm cognac over the shrimp and carefully ignite.
* Let the flames burn off while spooning the sauce over the shrimp.
= Make the Cream Sauce
* Pour in the heated heavy cream and bring the mixture to a gentle boil.
* Whisk in the flour to thicken the sauce, stirring continuously.
= Serve & Enjoy
* Remove from heat and serve immediately over rice, pasta, or with crusty bread.
. This Shrimp Tarragon is a luxurious dish perfect for impressing guests or treating yourself to a gourmet meal at home!
! The ingredient list calls for 5 tablespoons of butter, but the steps only use 4. The fifth tablespoon is never mentioned.

@ Norma's Meatballs
> These tender and flavorful meatballs are baked to perfection and coated in a sweet and tangy sauce. A family favorite, they make a great appetizer or main dish.
-: For the Meatballs
- 4 lbs lean ground beef
- 1 cup fine bread crumbs
- 1 cup milk
- 4 eggs
- Lots of fresh parsley, chopped
- ⅓ cup onion, finely chopped
- 2 teaspoons dry mustard
- 1 ½ teaspoons salt
-: For the Sauce
- 1 jar grape jelly
- 1 jar chili sauce
- Juice of 1 lemon
- A splash of sherry
= Preheat the Oven
* Set your oven to 350°F (175°C).
= Prepare the Meatballs
* In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, bread crumbs, milk, eggs, parsley, onion, dry mustard, and salt.
* Mix well until evenly incorporated.
* Shape the mixture into small meatballs and place them on a baking sheet.
= Bake
* Bake the meatballs for 25 minutes or until fully cooked and lightly browned.
= Prepare the Sauce
* In a saucepan, mix the grape jelly, chili sauce, lemon juice, and sherry.
* Heat over medium-low heat, stirring until the sauce is smooth and well combined.
= Combine and Serve
* Transfer the baked meatballs into a large pot or slow cooker.
* Pour the warm sauce over the meatballs and stir gently to coat.
* Simmer on low heat for a few minutes before serving.
. Enjoy these as a delicious appetizer or over rice for a hearty meal!

@ Basil Tomato Tart
> This savory basil tomato tart features juicy tomatoes, fresh basil, and a rich cheese filling in a flaky pie crust. It's perfect as a light lunch, appetizer, or side dish.
- 1 9-inch pie shell, pre-baked
- ½ cup shredded mozzarella
- 4–5 medium tomatoes
- 10 loosely packed basil leaves
- 4 cloves garlic
- ½ cup mayonnaise
- ¼ cup grated parmesan
- ⅛ teaspoon ground white pepper
= Preheat & Prepare Crust
* Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
* Pre-bake the pie crust according to package or recipe instructions.
* While still warm, sprinkle ½ cup mozzarella over the bottom and let it melt.
= Prepare the Tomatoes
* Slice tomatoes into wedges and drain on paper towels to remove excess moisture.
* Arrange the tomato slices over the melted cheese in the pie shell.
= Make the Basil Mixture
* In a food processor, combine basil, garlic, and ½ teaspoon olive oil until coarsely chopped.
* Sprinkle this mixture over the tomatoes.
= Prepare the Cheese Topping
* In a mixing bowl, combine the remaining mozzarella, mayonnaise, parmesan, and white pepper.
* Spoon the cheese mixture evenly over the tart.
= Bake & Serve
* Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 25–30 minutes, or until golden and bubbly.
* Let cool slightly before slicing.
. A perfect blend of fresh summer flavors and creamy cheese goodness!
! The steps use "the remaining mozzarella", but all ½ cup was already used on the crust. The original card probably called for more mozzarella. Olive oil is also used in the steps but missing from the ingredients.

@ Oysters Bienville
> A rich and flavorful dish featuring oysters baked with a creamy shrimp and bacon sauce!
- 4-5 slices bacon (cooked & chopped)
- ½ cup diced onion
- Salt & a pinch of cayenne
- 3 cloves garlic, minced (add late to prevent burning)
- 30g butter
- Flour (to make a thick roux)
- Sliced mushrooms
- 1 cup chopped raw shrimp
- White wine (for flavor)
- 2 egg yolks
= Prepare the Bacon & Aromatics
* Cook bacon until crispy, then chop.
* In the same pan, sauté onions with salt and cayenne.
* Add garlic towards the end to prevent burning.
= Make the Roux & Sauce
* In a separate pan, melt butter, then stir in flour gradually to form a thick roux.
* Slowly add sliced mushrooms, stirring constantly.
* Stir in chopped shrimp, followed by a splash of white wine.
* Simmer, letting the shrimp cook through.
* Remove from heat and whisk in egg yolks to thicken the sauce.
= Assemble & Bake
* Spoon the shrimp & bacon sauce over fresh oysters in their shells.
* Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 20 minutes, until bubbly and golden.
. A decadent and elegant seafood dish, perfect for special occasions!
! The oysters themselves are not in the ingredient list.

@ Frank's Crab Cakes
> These crispy, flavorful crab cakes are packed with delicate crab meat and a touch of spice. Pan-fried in butter and olive oil, they make a delicious appetizer or main course.
-: Per pound of crab meat
- 1 lb crab meat
- ½ teaspoon mustard powder
- ½ teaspoon celery salt
- Pinch of cayenne pepper
- ½ cup mayonnaise
- 1 egg
- Bread crumbs (for coating)
- Olive oil & butter (for frying)

* In a bowl, mix the mustard powder, celery salt, cayenne pepper, mayonnaise, and egg.
* Add the crab meat and mix gently until well combined.
= Form & Coat the Crab Cakes
* Shape the mixture into small patties.
* Roll each patty in bread crumbs, ensuring an even coating.
= Fry the Crab Cakes
* Heat a generous amount of olive oil and butter in a hot skillet over medium heat.
* Fry the crab cakes for 3–4 minutes per side, or until golden brown and crispy.
= Serve & Enjoy
* Serve hot with a squeeze of lemon or a creamy dipping sauce.
. Crispy on the outside and tender on the inside, perfect for any seafood lover!

# Salads
@ Roasted Fennel Salad
> A delicious blend of caramelized fennel, citrus, and crunchy pine nuts, perfect for a refreshing side dish or light meal. The roasting process enhances the natural sweetness of fennel, while the citrus zest and lemon juice provide a bright contrast.
- 1 fennel bulb
- Olive oil
- Salt & pepper
- 1 orange
- ½ small red onion
- 1 handful diced olives
- 1 handful pine nuts
- Lemon juice & zest (to taste)
= Prepare the fennel
* Trim off any tough outer layers and cut the fennel bulb into ½-inch thick slices. Reserve a handful of the fronds for garnish.
= Roast the fennel
* Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté fennel slices on both sides until caramelized.
* Season with salt and pepper. Set aside.
= Prepare the other ingredients
* Slice the red onion into paper-thin slices.
* Zest the orange and set aside.
* Remove the orange skin, then dice the fruit into bite-sized pieces.
= Assemble the salad
* Scatter the diced olives and pine nuts over the roasted fennel.
= Dress the salad
* Squeeze fresh lemon juice over the salad and drizzle with olive oil to taste.
= Garnish
* Sprinkle the reserved fennel fronds over the top and serve immediately.
. This salad pairs beautifully with grilled fish or roasted chicken.
! The orange, its zest and the red onion are prepared but never added to the salad in the steps.

@ Hunter's Chicken
> A rich and hearty dish featuring tender chicken simmered in a flavorful sauce made with mushrooms, white wine, beef stock, and herbs. Pairs well with rice, mashed potatoes, or crusty bread to soak up the sauce.
- 1 whole chicken, cut into pieces
- 2 tbsp butter
- ½ cup chopped onion
- 1 pkg mushrooms
- ½ cup white wine (cubist)
- ¼ cup beef stock
- 2 tbsp tomato paste (or ketchup)
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 3 tbsp cold water
- 1 tsp parsley
- 1 tsp tarragon
- Salt & pepper, to taste
= Cook the Chicken
* In a large skillet, melt the butter over medium heat.
* Add the chicken pieces and cook for 18-20 minutes until browned on all sides.
* Remove the chicken and set it aside.
= Prepare the Sauce
* In the same skillet, add the chopped onions and mushrooms.
* Season with salt and pepper.
* Cook for 4 minutes until softened.
= Deglaze and Simmer
* Add the white wine and let it cook for 3 minutes, allowing the alcohol to evaporate.
* Stir in the beef stock and tomato paste.
= Thicken the Sauce
* In a small bowl, mix cornstarch with cold water until smooth.
* Slowly add the cornstarch mixture to the skillet, stirring continuously.
= Finish Cooking
* Return the chicken to the pan and add the parsley and tarragon.
* Let the dish simmer until the sauce has thickened and the chicken is fully cooked.
= Serve
* Enjoy with rice, mashed potatoes, or a warm baguette.

- 2 tablespoons cornstarch
- ⅓ cup sugar
- 2 tablespoons flour
- 2 egg yolks
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 ½ tablespoons butter
= Make the Cake
* Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
* In a bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
* In a separate bowl, cream the butter, gradually adding the sugar while beating until light and fluffy.
* Beat in the egg.
* Alternately add the flour mixture and milk, beginning and ending with the flour.
* Stir in the vanilla last.
* Pour the batter into a greased cake pan and bake for 25 minutes or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
* Let the cake cool completely.
= Make the Filling
* Heat 1 ¾ cups of milk in a saucepan over medium heat.
* In a separate bowl, mix ¼ cup milk, cornstarch, sugar, and flour until smooth.
* Add the egg yolks to the mixture and whisk well.
* Gradually pour this mixture into the hot milk, stirring constantly.
* Cook over medium heat, stirring continuously, until the mixture thickens.
* Remove from heat and stir in vanilla and butter.
= Cool and Assemble
* Transfer the filling to a bowl, cover with wax paper, and refrigerate until cool.
* Once the cake and filling are cool, slice the cake in half and spread the filling between the layers.

@ Jam Tarts
> A flaky, buttery crust filled with sweet raspberry jam. A perfect treat for tea time or dessert, especially with a dollop of whipped cream.
- ¾ cup shortening
- ¼ cup sugar
- 1 egg, unbeaten
- 1 tablespoon milk
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 1 ½ cups flour
- ½ teaspoon vanilla
- Raspberry jam (for filling)
= Prepare the Dough
* In a large bowl, mix the shortening and sugar until well combined.
* Add the egg, milk, vanilla, salt, and flour. Mix until a dough forms.
= Roll & Cut
* Roll out the dough on a lightly floured surface.
* Cut rounds using a cookie cutter or a glass to fit into tartlet molds.
= Fill the Tarts
* Place the dough rounds into a greased tartlet pan.
* Fill each tart with a spoonful of raspberry jam.
= Bake
* Conventional oven: bake at 350°F (175°C) for 20 minutes.
* Convection oven: bake at 325°F (163°C) for 16 minutes.
= Serve & Enjoy
* Allow the tarts to cool slightly before removing them from the pan.
* Serve with a dollop of Cool Whip or whipped cream.

@ Christmas Pineapple Cake
> A rich, fruit-filled holiday treat with coconut, raisins, and cherries. It bakes low and slow for a moist and flavorful finish.
- 3 eggs, separated
- 1 ¾ cups white sugar
- 1 cup butter
- 1 lb white raisins
- 2 ¾ cups flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- ¼ cup grated orange rind
- 1 lb mixed red & green cherries
- 1 (20 oz) can crushed pineapple
- 8 oz candied pineapple
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- ½ teaspoon nutmeg
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup coconut
= Prepare the Batter
* Cream the butter and sugar together until light and fluffy.
* Add the egg yolks one at a time, mixing well after each addition.
= Add Dry Ingredients
* In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, salt, and nutmeg.
* Gradually add the dry ingredients to the butter mixture, mixing until combined.
= Incorporate the Fruits
* Stir in the grated orange rind, crushed pineapple, coconut, raisins, cherries, and candied pineapple.
= Fold in the Egg Whites
* In a clean bowl, beat the egg whites until stiff peaks form.
* Gently fold the egg whites into the batter to keep the cake light.
= Bake
* Grease a loaf or bundt cake pan and pour in the batter.
* Bake at 300°F (150°C) for 2 hours until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
= Cool & Serve
* Let the cake cool completely before removing it from the pan.
* Slice and enjoy as a festive Christmas treat!
! Vanilla is listed but never used in the steps.

@ Nannie's 1-Egg Cake
> Simple, classic, light and fluffy. A no-fuss recipe that needs just one egg, great for a quick homemade treat.
- 1 ¾ cups flour
- 3 ½ teaspoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ⅓ cup butter or shortening
- 1 scant cup of white sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 cup sweet milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
= Prepare the Batter
* Cream the butter or shortening until smooth.
* Gradually add sugar, beating well between additions. The fine texture of the cake depends on proper beating.
* Beat the egg separately and combine it with the butter-sugar mixture.
= Combine Ingredients
* Mix the dry ingredients (flour, baking powder, and salt) in a separate bowl.
* Add the dry mixture to the wet ingredients alternately with the milk.
* Stir in the vanilla last.
= Bake
* Pour the batter into a greased cake pan.
* Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 25 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
. Let the cake cool before serving. Enjoy it on its own, or frost it with your favorite icing!

@ Lemon Sorbet
> The perfect light and tangy dessert for hot summer days or as a palate cleanser between courses. Smooth, bright, and made with just a few ingredients.
- 1 cup milk (2% or skim is fine)
- 1 cup whipping cream
- 5 lemons (to yield 1 cup fresh lemon juice)
- ⅔ cup berry sugar (fine granulated sugar)
= Prepare the Mixture
* Squeeze the lemons to extract 1 cup of fresh lemon juice.
* In a mixing bowl, combine the lemon juice, milk, whipping cream, and berry sugar. Stir well until the sugar is fully dissolved.
= Freeze the Sorbet
* Pour the mixture into a shallow dish and place it in the freezer.
* Freeze for about 4 hours, stirring occasionally to break up ice crystals and create a smooth texture.
= Final Touches
* Once the sorbet reaches a slushy consistency, spoon it into serving dishes (such as lemon halves for a decorative touch).
* Return the sorbet to the freezer until ready to serve.

@ Rice Pudding
> A comforting, creamy dessert that's simple to make and perfect for any time of year. A hint of vanilla and a touch of nutmeg make this baked version rich and satisfying.
- 2 eggs
- 1 ½ cups milk
- ¾ cup cooked rice
- ¼ cup brown sugar
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- Nutmeg (for topping, optional)
= Preheat & Prepare
* Preheat oven to 325°F (160°C).
* Grease a casserole dish.
= Mix Ingredients
* In a medium bowl, beat the eggs.
* Stir in the milk, cooked rice, brown sugar, salt, and vanilla until well combined.
= Bake
* Pour the mixture into the prepared casserole dish.
* Place the dish in a larger pan filled with hot water (water bath) to help it cook evenly.
* Bake for about 45 minutes, stirring once during baking if desired.
= Finish & Serve
* If desired, sprinkle nutmeg on top before serving.
* Serve warm or chilled.
